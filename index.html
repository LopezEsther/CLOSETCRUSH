The Story

Secondhand first. Baddie always.

ClosetCrush started as one girl's rack of thrifted finds she couldn't fit in her own closet anymore. Now it's a rotating edit of pre-loved pieces, sourced and styled for people who want main-character fits without the price tag — or the waste.

Every piece is checked, cleaned, and picked because it deserves a second life. Sustainable style isn't a trend here, it's the whole point.
